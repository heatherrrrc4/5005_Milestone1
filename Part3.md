Data Visualization
After I explored my YouTube watch history dataset, I created visualizations to better understand my viewing behavior.

Visualization 1: Title Length Distribution
I also analyzed the length of video titles. The histogram shows that most titles are around 40–50 characters long, 
with some longer titles. This reflects how different types of content (e.g., ambient music vs. ads or 
entertainment videos) use different naming strategies.

Visualization 2: Content Categories
I categorized videos into three groups: Cozy/Sleep, Music, and Other. The bar chart shows that most of my viewing 
activity falls into the "Other" category, while cozy/sleep-related content appears frequently but still represents 
a smaller portion. This suggests that while I intentionally search for relaxing content, my overall consumption is 
still more diverse.

Insights
One interesting finding is that there are very few ads in the dataset, and there is little evidence of 
recommendation-driven content. This may be because I was not logged into a YouTube account when watching these 
videos, limiting personalized tracking. Overall, the dataset reflects a mix of intentional and casual viewing behavior.
